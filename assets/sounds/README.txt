GUTTER SAINTS AUDIO PLACEHOLDERS

Drop your own licensed/local MP3/OGG/WAV effects in this folder using the filenames in ../audio.json.
No copyrighted audio is bundled.

Suggested files:
ball_roll.mp3, pins.mp3, strike_sting.mp3, spare_sting.mp3, crowd_roar.mp3,
crowd_gasp.mp3, crowd_groan.mp3, streak_sting.mp3, perfect_tension.mp3,
champion_fanfare.mp3, entrance_sting.mp3

Voice playback requires FFmpeg installed and available on PATH plus PyNaCl (included in requirements.txt).
The bot only plays sounds when it is connected to a voice channel.
