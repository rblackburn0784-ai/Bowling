# Gutter Saints Bowling v2.9.1 — The Show Must Go On

**Base:** v2.9.0 (`main` at `336433e4c2c2253cf0a9b3f0d792a731e06a72bc`).

## Highlights

### 1. Final stage-by-stage commentary

- Keeps the existing 25 non-repeating Dude-inspired combinations per stage/event.
- Adds delivery-aware (first ball, second ball, tenth frame and finals) cues for **Approach → Travel → Breakpoint → Impact**.
- Stage cues contain no future pinfall, scores, splits, spare results or strike declarations. Commentary for an outcome is emitted only once the GIF has completed.
- Dedicated accurate post-delivery calls for **7–10**, **Big Four**, **Greek Church**, split conversions, **turkey**, six-pack, front nine, eleven consecutive strikes, **300**, tenth-frame spares, messenger, Brooklyn, ringing ten and stone eight/nine.
- Fixes `0/` after a gutter being called a strike in the play-by-play. No scoring logic is changed.
- Moment headlines are stable when Discord redraws a delivery. All editorial choices are independent of `GameSession.rng`.

### 2. Character reactions and winner celebrations

- On the settled, *post-delivery* lane image, character poses switch to the optional `cheer.png` for strikes, streaks and split conversions. The original five approach PNG frames and timing are unchanged.
- A missing cheer pose falls back to existing fifth release sprite. No art is invented, and the result stays playable with missing assets.
- Unique match winner continues to receive the existing victory pose; drawn team/solo matches do not arbitrarily crown a winner.

### 3. Broadcast transitions

- Subtle **Approach / Ball in Flight / Breakpoint / On the Deck** phase ribbons appear in the upper overhead camera viewport during the existing one-upload animation. Pins lie below that reserved ribbon area.
- Retains the established **850×750, 17:15 hybrid graphic**, the left photographic hero lane, regulation 4–3–2–1 overhead deck and square pit view.
- No new network edits per GIF frame; no camera coordinate or animation duration changes (original **2.795 seconds**).

### 4. Introductions and finales

- Locally drawn **850×750 PNG introduction slate** presents team line-ups, individual entrants, lane numbers, lane oil and the match format. It needs no additional asset pack.
- Added to **fresh** `/game_start`, Match Setup exhibition, friendly challenge acceptance and competition fixtures. Undo/Restore do **not** replay introductions.
- Competition fixture launching now posts the live bowling controls after the announcement.
- Finals and competition games receive a separate match-final card after the **committed** result. The existing winner celebration and scoreboard are preserved.
- `/game_start` has an optional **layout** choice: Standard, Broadcast, Finals or Chaos.
- Automatic Finals presentation for grand finals and the single last bracket fixture in an elimination event. Ordinary round robin matches are not incorrectly labelled finals.

### 5. Championship atmosphere

- Gold finals treatment, finals-specific commentary cues, occasional context-aware crowd beats in the existing Game Updates field (rather than flooding the channel) and optional sound stings using existing audio configuration.
- Crowd lines are throttled to once per four delivered balls. Audio/media remain optional and silent if assets or a voice connection are missing.
- Existing honours, Gazette, advancement and progression systems remain unchanged.

## Safety and installation

- **No database migration or changed SQLite tables.** Keep your existing `data/gutter_saints.db` and back it up before installing.
- No changes to the physics algorithm, pinfall engine, oil model, shot decisions, scoring rules or animation frame timings.
- The archive intentionally does **not** contain production PNG assets from the operator's installation. Preserve `assets/lane`, `assets/cameras`, `assets/pins`, `assets/sprites`, `assets/balls` and any configured sounds/GIF URLs.
- `assets/sprites/the_dude/cheer.png` and `assets/sprites/jesus/cheer.png` improve celebrations but are optional; the existing fallback is retained.
- The **production-art alignment** remains a live-server acceptance task: clean overhead photographic plate, hero character anchors, pit pin scaling and GIF playback on Discord mobile/desktop.
